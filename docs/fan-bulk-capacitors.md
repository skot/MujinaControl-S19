# Fan supply bulk capacitors

C289 and C290 are Panasonic EEHZC1E101XP (catalog C178640), 100 uF ±20%, 25 V hybrid aluminum electrolytics. Both positive pins connect to protected +12V_FAN downstream of Q1; negative pins connect to GND. Existing ceramics C283–C285 are retained. C289 serves fans 1–2, C290 fans 3–4.

The manufacturer specifies 30 milliohm maximum ESR and 1.4 A RMS ripple at 100 kHz, -55 to +125 C operation, and 4000 h endurance at 125 C. Ripple rating is frequency-dependent; the catalog's 210 mA at 100 Hz is not the 100 kHz rating. These capacitors reduce fast supply disturbances; they do not supply sustained fan startup current. Check rail transients and capacitor heating with the actual fans and supply, including simultaneous start and hot plug. Added nominal stored energy at 12 V is 14.4 mJ total; no controlled-inrush function is added.
